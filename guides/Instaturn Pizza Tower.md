So, you finally wanna learn how to speedrun in Pizza Tower?

Great, first thing you're gonna learn is **Instaturning.**

What's an instaturn? It's a tech that makes you turn almost instantly.

There are also 2 types of an Instaturn:
- Taunt-buffered Instaturn
- Pause-buffered instaturn

How to do it?

First, you must be on a **Fast Walk** state. To do a Fast Walk, While in mach 3 or higher, 
Press **Jump** and immediately press **Grab**, and release **Shift**. You're now in 
Fast Walk. 

To do a Taunt-buffered instaturn, Get in A Fast Walk, press **Taunt**, and immediately press the **opposite direction** and **Shift**.

To do a Pause-buffered Instaturn, Get in a Fast Walk, press **Pause**, and immediately press the **opposite direction**, **Shift**, then **Unpause**.

Now, you've learned how to do a Instaturn, with Fast Walk.
