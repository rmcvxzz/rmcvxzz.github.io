Here's a recipe for the sacred **<u>Remspecial:</u>**

- 2 Slices whole grain
- Natural almond
- 1/2 Banana
- 1 Tsp of honey
- A dash of cinnamon
- Greek yogurt (any)
- Hemp seeds

Simply put all of them into a sandwich.

This special sandwich is not only tasty, but mostly healthy and will keep you energized for a long time.
